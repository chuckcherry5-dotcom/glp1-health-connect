How We Review GLP-1 Programs

Our goal is to provide independent, transparent analysis of GLP-1 telehealth programs. Here's how we evaluate them.

Our Evaluation Criteria

Medical Oversight
Do they employ licensed providers? Is there proper medical screening? Are check-ins regular and meaningful?
Transparency
Are costs clearly stated upfront? Do they disclose affiliate relationships? Are terms and conditions clear?
Safety Practices
Do they screen for contraindications? Do they address side effects? Is there emergency support?
Pricing & Value
Is pricing competitive? What's actually included? Are there hidden fees?
Patient Experience
Is customer support responsive? Is the platform easy to use? Are patients satisfied?

What We Don't Do

  • Accept payments to promote programs
  • Rank programs based on commission rates
  • Hide negative information about high-commission programs
  • Guarantee any program's safety or efficacy

About Our Affiliate Model

GLP-1 Health Connect earns affiliate commissions when users enroll through our links. This supports our ability to provide free, independent research. Our evaluations are not influenced by commission rates. See our Affiliate Disclosure for details.

Limitations of Our Review

We evaluate programs based on publicly available information, our research, and reported user experiences. We cannot:

  • Verify every provider's credentials individually
  • Guarantee a program's future safety or quality
  • Predict your individual outcome or experience

Always conduct your own due diligence and consult your healthcare provider before enrolling.
