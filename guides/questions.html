Questions to Ask Before Choosing a GLP-1 Program

Use this checklist when evaluating GLP-1 telehealth programs to ensure you're making the right choice.

About the Program

How long has this program been operating?
Look for established programs with track records of serving patients safely.
Are the providers licensed and credentialed?
Verify providers are MDs, DOs, NPs, or PAs with proper medical licensing.
What medications do you offer?
Ensure they offer semaglutide, tirzepatide, or other options you're considering.

About Medical Care

What screening do you do for contraindications?
Good programs screen for thyroid cancer history, pancreatitis, and other conditions.
How often will I speak with a provider?
Look for at least monthly check-ins, not just automated messages.
What's the process if I experience side effects?
Clear protocols should exist for managing nausea, vomiting, and other side effects.

About Costs and Logistics

What are all the costs? (consultation + medication)
Get a complete breakdown before committing.
Do you work with insurance?
Some programs accept insurance; others require out-of-pocket payment.
What's the cancellation policy?
Understand how to stop if needed and any cancellation fees.

Red Flags to Avoid

  • Providers who don't conduct proper medical evaluations
  • Programs that guarantee results or specific weight loss
  • No clear communication channels or support
  • Very low prices with unclear what's included
  • No mention of potential side effects or risks
