GLP-1 Program Costs: What to Expect

Understanding costs is crucial before starting a GLP-1 program. Prices vary significantly based on the program, medication, and insurance.

Typical Program Costs

Without Insurance: Most programs range from $99–$299 per month for consultations plus medication costs of $500–$1,500+ monthly.

What's Included in Costs?

  • Initial medical evaluation ($0–$150)
  • Ongoing provider oversight ($50–$150/month)
  • Medication cost ($500–$1,500+/month)
  • Optional: Nutritional counseling and support

Ways to Reduce Costs

  • Check if your insurance covers telehealth programs
  • Look for patient assistance programs
  • Compare programs for best pricing
  • Ask about bundle discounts

Always verify current pricing directly with programs before enrolling.
