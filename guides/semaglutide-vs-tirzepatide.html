Semaglutide vs Tirzepatide: A Complete Comparison

Last updated: August 2026 | Sourced from FDA and NIH publications

Both semaglutide and tirzepatide are effective GLP-1 medications. Tirzepatide may offer greater weight loss on average, but the best choice depends on your individual health profile.

What Are These Medications?

Semaglutide (Ozempic®, Wegovy®) is a GLP-1 receptor agonist that mimics a hormone regulating appetite and blood sugar.

Tirzepatide (Mounjaro®) is a dual agonist targeting both GLP-1 and GIP receptors for enhanced effects.

How They Work

Semaglutide

  • Reduces appetite
  • Slows gastric emptying
  • Improves insulin secretion
  • Regulates blood sugar

Tirzepatide

  • Same as semaglutide, plus:
  • Enhances insulin response to meals
  • Reduces glucagon release
  • May provide additional weight loss

Common Side Effects

  • Nausea (especially starting)
  • Vomiting
  • Diarrhea/constipation
  • Stomach pain
  • Fatigue

The Bottom Line

Both medications are effective. Tirzepatide may offer slightly greater weight loss. Discuss options with your provider to determine the best choice for you.
