How Online GLP-1 Telehealth Programs Work

GLP-1 telehealth programs combine online consultations with medication delivery to your home. Here's the typical process:

The Process: 7 Steps

1 Sign Up
Create an account on the program's website. Provide basic information and pay any upfront fees.
2 Medical Questionnaire
Complete a detailed health history. Answer questions about medications, conditions, and lifestyle.
3 Provider Review
A licensed provider reviews your information and screens for contraindications.
4 Initial Consultation
Video or phone call with a provider to discuss goals, answer questions, and confirm medication choice.
5 Prescription & Delivery
If approved, a prescription is sent to the program's partner pharmacy. Medication ships to your home.
6 Start Treatment
Begin injections at home following provided instructions. Most start with a low dose and increase gradually.
7 Ongoing Care
Regular check-ins with your provider (typically monthly). Dose adjustments, monitoring, and support as needed.

Key Advantages

  • Convenient—no office visits needed
  • Medication delivered to your home
  • Often more affordable than traditional clinics
  • Regular provider oversight
  • Flexible scheduling

Important Notes

Not all programs are the same. Some prioritize medical oversight; others prioritize speed. Use our guides to evaluate programs carefully before enrolling.
