What's Included in GLP-1 Telehealth Programs

Most quality GLP-1 telehealth programs include these essential components:

Medical Evaluation & Ongoing Care

  • Initial health history and eligibility review
  • Regular check-ins with licensed providers (typically monthly)
  • Medication monitoring and adjustment
  • Emergency support for side effects or concerns

Medication & Delivery

  • FDA-approved GLP-1 medications
  • Home delivery of injectable medications
  • Injection supplies and instructions
  • Pharmacy coordination

Support Services (varies by program)

  • Nutritional guidance and meal planning
  • Behavioral health support
  • Community support groups or forums
  • Progress tracking tools and dashboards

Red Flags: What Should Be Included

Before enrolling, verify that your program includes:

  • Licensed, credentialed healthcare providers
  • Regular provider contact (not just automation)
  • Proper medical screening for contraindications
  • Clear communication channels for concerns
