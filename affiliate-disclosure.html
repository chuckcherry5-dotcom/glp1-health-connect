Affiliate Disclosure

Transparency Statement: GLP-1 Health Connect earns affiliate commissions when users enroll through our links. This supports our free educational resource. However, our program evaluations are independent and not influenced by commission rates.

How We Make Money

GLP-1 Health Connect is free. We cover costs through affiliate commissions when users enroll through our links. This does not cost you extra—you pay the same whether you enroll through our link or directly.

How This Affects Our Reviews

Our affiliate relationships do not influence our program evaluations:

  • We do not rank programs based on commission amounts
  • We do not promote lower-quality programs that pay higher commissions
  • We do not exclude programs that pay lower commissions
  • We do not suppress negative information about high-commission programs

Our Commitment to Honesty

We evaluate programs based on objective criteria: medical oversight, transparency, pricing, quality of support, and program reliability. Affiliate commissions do not change this.
