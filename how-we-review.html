Our Review Methodology

At GLP-1 Health Connect, we believe transparency is essential. This page explains how we research, evaluate, and compare GLP-1 telehealth programs.

Our Commitment to Independence

We evaluate programs based on objective criteria, not based on which programs pay us the most. While we earn affiliate commissions when users enroll in programs through our links, these commissions do not influence our program rankings or recommendations.

Evaluation Criteria

  • Medical Oversight & Safety - Are providers licensed and do they supervise medications?
  • Transparency & Honesty - Are pricing and claims clearly disclosed?
  • Program Quality & Support - What support services are provided?
  • Medication & Delivery - What medications are offered and how reliable is delivery?
  • Cost & Value - Are prices competitive with similar programs?

Our Research Process

  • Direct communication with program representatives
  • Review of program websites, terms of service, and pricing
  • Analysis of provider credentials and licensing
  • Review of medical literature and clinical evidence
