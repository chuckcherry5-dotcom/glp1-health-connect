What's Included in Telehealth GLP-1 Programs

Last updated: August 2026 | Program components checklist

Before enrolling in a GLP-1 telehealth program, it's important to understand what you'll receive. Quality programs should include medical oversight, ongoing support, and clear communication about your treatment plan.

✓ Communication & Access

Your First Visit: What to Expect

Step 1: Online Intake (5-10 minutes)

Complete a health questionnaire with medical history, allergies, medications, and lifestyle information.

Step 2: Provider Consultation (15-30 minutes)

Video or phone call with a licensed provider who reviews your health, discusses your goals, and determines if you're a candidate for GLP-1 therapy.

Step 3: Treatment Plan

If approved, the provider creates a treatment plan including your starting dose, follow-up schedule, and any special instructions.

Step 4: Prescription & Medication Ordering

Your prescription is sent to the pharmacy, and your medication is ordered and shipped to you.

Step 5: Medication Delivery

You receive your medication with injection supplies and instructions. Your first follow-up appointment is typically scheduled for 2-4 weeks later.

Ongoing Appointments & Check-ins

During the First 3 Months (Dose Escalation Phase)

  • Frequency: Monthly check-ins (usually via telehealth)
  • What happens: Provider reviews side effects, adjusts dose, answers questions
  • Duration: Typically 15-20 minutes per appointment
  • Cost: Included in program fee

After Reaching Maintenance Dose (3+ Months)

  • Frequency: Quarterly (every 3 months) or as needed
  • What happens: Weight and progress check-ins, medication refills, ongoing support
  • Duration: Typically 10-15 minutes per appointment
  • Cost: Included in program fee

Questions to Ask During Your First Consultation

Before starting, make sure you understand these details:

  • How often will I have follow-up appointments?
  • What's the process for dose increases?
  • How do I reach support if I have problems or side effects?
  • Will lab work or bloodwork be required? At what cost?
  • What happens if I want to pause or stop the program?
  • Can I switch medications if one doesn't work for me?
  • How long will I need to stay on the medication?
  • Are there any additional costs beyond the monthly program fee?

Red Flags: What to Watch For

Be cautious if a program:

  • Doesn't require an initial medical consultation or health assessment
  • Doesn't provide ongoing medical oversight or follow-up
  • Guarantees specific weight loss results
  • Doesn't screen for medical contraindications
  • Makes unrealistic claims about the medication
  • Has no customer support or only automated responses
  • Doesn't provide clear communication about side effects and risks
Important: Quality GLP-1 programs always include medical oversight from a licensed healthcare provider. Avoid programs that dispense medication without proper medical evaluation and ongoing supervision.

The Bottom Line

A good GLP-1 telehealth program should provide comprehensive medical care, clear communication, and ongoing support. Don't settle for less than a program that includes regular provider check-ins, side effect management, and access to support between appointments.

The program you choose should feel like a partnership with your healthcare provider—not just a prescription service.
