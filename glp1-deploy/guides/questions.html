Essential Questions Before Choosing a GLP-1 Provider

Last updated: August 2026 | Evaluation and comparison guide

Choosing a GLP-1 telehealth provider is an important health decision. Before committing, it's essential to ask the right questions to ensure you select a program that's reputable, safe, and right for your needs.

Questions About Medical Oversight

Medical Credentials & Supervision

  • Are your providers licensed physicians, nurse practitioners, or physician assistants?
  • Are providers licensed in my state?
  • Do physicians review and supervise all prescriptions?
  • Is there a specific provider I'll work with, or does it vary?
  • Can I request a provider with specific experience or specialties?

Initial Evaluation & Assessment

  • Will I have a consultation before being prescribed medication?
  • How thorough is the health screening process?
  • Do you check for contraindications or conditions that would make GLP-1 unsafe for me?
  • Will you review my current medications for potential interactions?
  • What if I'm not approved—is there a refund or credit?

Ongoing Medical Support

  • How often will I have follow-up appointments after starting?
  • Can I reach a provider if I experience side effects between appointments?
  • How are dose adjustments made, and how often?
  • Will you monitor for side effects or complications?
  • Can I message my provider, or only schedule formal appointments?

Questions About Medication & Costs

Pricing & Fees

  • What's the exact cost breakdown (membership, medication, shipping)?
  • Are there hidden fees I should know about?
  • What happens to the cost when I move to higher doses?
  • Do you offer patient assistance programs or discounts?
  • Can I pay out-of-pocket or must I use a specific payment method?

Medication Selection & Options

  • What medications do you offer (semaglutide, tirzepatide, etc.)?
  • Can I request a specific medication or brand?
  • If I have problems with one medication, can I switch to another?
  • Do you prescribe generic or brand-name medications?
  • How much does it cost to switch medications?

Insurance & Coverage

  • Do you work with insurance, or is it cash pay only?
  • Will you submit claims to my insurance?
  • Can you help me understand my insurance coverage?
  • What's your policy if insurance denies coverage?

Questions About Program Quality & Support

Customer Support & Communication

  • What hours is customer support available?
  • How do I contact support (phone, email, chat)?
  • What's the typical response time for questions?
  • Is there 24/7 emergency support if I have serious side effects?
  • Do you have a patient app or online portal for scheduling?

Education & Support Services

  • Do you provide education about the medication and how to inject it?
  • Is nutrition or lifestyle coaching included?
  • Are there community support groups or coaching calls?
  • Do you provide resources about side effects and how to manage them?

Medication Delivery & Logistics

  • How quickly will I receive my medication after approval?
  • Is shipping included, or is there a separate cost?
  • What's your policy if medication is lost or damaged in transit?
  • Do you provide needles, syringes, and other injection supplies?
  • Where will the medication be shipped from (what state)?

Questions About Policies & Commitments

Commitment & Cancellation Policies

  • Are there any minimum commitment periods?
  • Can I cancel anytime, or are there penalties?
  • Can I pause my subscription temporarily?
  • What happens to my unused medication if I stop?
  • Will you refund any unused portions of my payment?

Transparency & Disclaimers

  • Do you clearly explain the risks and potential side effects?
  • Do you disclose that this is off-label use (for weight management)?
  • Are there any contraindications I should know about?
  • Do you provide a clear medical disclaimer?
  • Are your marketing claims accurate and evidence-based?

Company Background & Credibility

  • How long has the company been in business?
  • Are they listed with the Better Business Bureau or similar?
  • Do independent reviews or testimonials exist from verified patients?
  • Have they been subject to regulatory actions or warnings?
  • Are they transparent about how they use affiliate marketing (if applicable)?

Red Flags: What to Avoid

Don't use a program if:

  • They don't require an initial medical consultation
  • They guarantee specific weight loss results
  • They don't screen for contraindications
  • They have no customer support or responsive communication
  • They avoid discussing risks and side effects
  • They pressure you to commit long-term without a trial period
  • They're not transparent about their affiliate relationships
  • They operate without proper medical licensing or oversight

Final Recommendation

Take time to evaluate multiple programs. Ask these questions, compare answers, and choose the provider that best aligns with your needs. The "cheapest" option isn't always the best—prioritize medical oversight, support quality, and transparency.

A good GLP-1 telehealth program should feel like a genuine partnership with healthcare professionals who care about your safety and success.
