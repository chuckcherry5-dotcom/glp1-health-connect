Understanding GLP-1 Program Costs

Last updated: August 2026 | Guide to budgeting for GLP-1 treatment

Cost is one of the biggest questions when considering a GLP-1 telehealth program. These medications and programs aren't cheap, but understanding the pricing structure can help you budget and find the most affordable option for your situation.

Total Cost Breakdown

When budgeting for a GLP-1 program, costs typically fall into these categories:

1. Program Membership Fee

Range: $0–$150/month

Some programs charge a monthly membership, while others build this into the medication cost. This covers access to the provider, consultations, and program management.

2. Medication Cost

Range: $400–$1,500/month

This is the largest expense. Prices vary significantly based on:

  • Medication type (semaglutide vs. tirzepatide)
  • Dose (starting doses are cheaper than maximum doses)
  • Generic vs. brand-name
  • Program partnerships and discounts

3. Shipping & Handling

Range: $0–$50/month

Most programs include shipping, but some charge separately or add a small handling fee.

4. Optional Costs

Examples: Lab work, additional consultations, weight loss support programs

Some programs offer additional services for an extra fee.

Insurance vs. Cash Pay

Using Insurance

  • Pros: May cover 80–90% of costs if medication is approved; some insurance plans have preferred programs
  • Cons: Requires prior authorization; may be denied for off-label weight management use; high out-of-pocket maximums
  • Reality: Insurance coverage for weight management is inconsistent. Some plans cover it; many don't

Cash Pay

  • Pros: No insurance approval needed; faster access; programs offer patient assistance and discounts
  • Cons: Full out-of-pocket cost; can be expensive long-term
  • Reality: Most people using telehealth GLP-1 programs pay cash or use patient assistance programs

Cost Considerations Checklist

Ways to Save Money

1. Patient Assistance Programs

Pharmaceutical manufacturers offer patient assistance programs that can reduce or eliminate medication costs for eligible individuals. Ask your telehealth provider about availability.

2. Compare Program Pricing

Prices vary significantly between programs. Getting quotes from multiple providers can save $50–$200/month.

3. Start with a Lower Dose

Starting doses are typically less expensive than maintenance doses. You can build up gradually, spreading costs over time.

4. Verify Insurance Coverage

Even if you typically pay cash, check with your insurance. Some plans cover GLP-1 medications for weight management.

5. Consider Generic Options

As patents expire, generic versions may become available at lower costs. Ask if your program offers generics.

6. Prescription Discount Cards

Services like GoodRx, SingleCare, or RxSaver can sometimes reduce medication costs, even for telehealth programs.

Annual Cost Estimates

Annual budget planning: Most people on GLP-1 programs budget approximately $1,500–$2,000 per year on medication and program fees, though costs can be higher with premium programs or higher doses.

That works out to roughly:

  • Low-cost programs: ~$1,500–$1,800/year
  • Mid-range programs: ~$2,000–$3,000/year
  • Premium programs: ~$3,000+/year

Questions to Ask Before Starting

When evaluating a program, always ask:

  • What's the exact cost breakdown (membership, medication, shipping)?
  • Are there discounts for paying upfront or paying in full?
  • What's the price increase when I move to higher doses?
  • Can I cancel anytime, or are there minimum commitment periods?
  • Are there any hidden fees or add-ons I should know about?
  • Does the program work with insurance, or is it cash pay only?
  • Are patient assistance programs available?
  • What's the refund or return policy if I'm not satisfied?

The Bottom Line

GLP-1 programs are an investment in your health. While costs can be significant, most telehealth programs are more affordable than traditional in-person weight loss clinics or concierge medicine. By understanding the pricing structure and comparing options, you can find a program that fits your budget and health goals.

Always verify current pricing directly with each provider—prices change frequently and vary by location and medication type.
