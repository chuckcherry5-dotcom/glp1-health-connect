Before Starting Any Treatment

Before enrolling in a GLP-1 program or starting any medication:

  • Consult with your primary care physician
  • Disclose your complete medical history
  • List all current medications and supplements
  • Discuss potential risks and side effects
  • Ask about contraindications specific to your health
  • Review the telehealth provider's credentials and experience
  • Understand the program's terms, costs, and cancellation policy

Emergency Situations

If you experience symptoms that could indicate a medical emergency (chest pain, severe abdominal pain, difficulty breathing, signs of stroke), call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room immediately. Do not rely on telehealth or information from this site in emergency situations.

Pregnant or Breastfeeding

If you are pregnant, breastfeeding, or planning to become pregnant, GLP-1 medications may not be appropriate. Discuss this with your healthcare provider before starting any treatment.
