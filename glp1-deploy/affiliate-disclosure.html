Affiliate Disclosure

Last updated: August 2026

Transparency Statement: GLP-1 Health Connect earns affiliate commissions when users enroll in programs through our referral links. This supports our ability to maintain this free educational resource. However, our program evaluations and recommendations are independent and not influenced by commission rates.

How We Make Money

GLP-1 Health Connect is a free resource. We cover operational costs through affiliate commissions earned when users enroll in programs featured on our site through our links.

What This Means

When you click on a program link and complete enrollment, we may receive a commission. This does not cost you anything extra—you pay the same price whether you enroll through our link or directly.

How This Affects Our Reviews

Our affiliate relationships do not influence our program evaluations in the following ways:

  • We do not rank programs based on commission amounts
  • We do not promote lower-quality programs because they pay higher commissions
  • We do not exclude programs that pay lower commissions
  • We do not suppress negative information about high-commission programs

Our Commitment to Honesty

We evaluate programs based on objective criteria: medical oversight, transparency, pricing, quality of support, and program reliability.

If a program offers a higher commission but has poor medical oversight or misleading claims, we will note these concerns in our review.

Conversely, if a program offers lower commissions but provides excellent medical care and transparency, we will recommend it based on those merits.

What We Don't Accept

We do not accept:

  • Paid advertising or sponsored placements
  • Money directly from programs to promote them
  • Payments to hide negative information
  • Exclusive partnerships that bias our coverage

Affiliate commissions are our only financial relationship with featured programs.

Your Benefit

This model allows us to maintain GLP-1 Health Connect as a free, ad-free resource. You benefit from honest, independent research without paywall barriers.

How to Enroll Directly

If you prefer to enroll directly without using our link, that's perfectly fine. Simply visit the program's website directly and complete enrollment there. You'll receive the same service and pricing.

Questions?

If you have questions about our affiliate relationships or believe we've compromised our editorial integrity, contact us. We take transparency seriously and welcome feedback.

FTC Compliance

This site discloses affiliate relationships in compliance with FTC guidelines. We clearly identify when links may result in affiliate commissions.
