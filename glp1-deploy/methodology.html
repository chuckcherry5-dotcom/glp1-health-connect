Our Review Methodology

At GLP-1 Health Connect, we believe transparency is essential. This page explains how we research, evaluate, and compare GLP-1 telehealth programs to help you make informed decisions.

Our Commitment to Independence

We evaluate programs based on objective criteria, not based on which programs pay us the most. While we earn affiliate commissions when users enroll in programs through our links, these commissions do not influence our program rankings or recommendations.

Our goal is to provide honest, evidence-based information that serves your interests—not to promote specific programs.

Evaluation Criteria

Medical Oversight & Safety

  • Are providers licensed and credentialed in the states they serve?
  • Does the program require an initial medical consultation?
  • Is there ongoing medical supervision and regular follow-up?
  • Do they screen for contraindications and safety concerns?
  • Is there emergency support if complications occur?

Transparency & Honesty

  • Are pricing and costs clearly disclosed upfront?
  • Do they accurately represent medications, efficacy, and side effects?
  • Are they transparent about affiliate relationships?
  • Do they avoid unrealistic guarantees or exaggerated claims?
  • Are terms of service clear and fair?

Program Quality & Support

  • What support do they provide (consultations, education, coaching)?
  • How responsive is their customer service?
  • Is there a patient app or portal for communication?
  • Do they offer options for dose adjustment or medication switching?
  • What's the cancellation and refund policy?

Medication & Delivery

  • What medications do they offer?
  • Is shipping included and reliable?
  • Are injection supplies provided?
  • What happens if medication is lost or damaged?
  • Is there flexibility in delivery schedules?

Cost & Value

  • Are prices competitive with similar programs?
  • Do they offer payment plans or assistance programs?
  • Is the value proposition clear (what you get for your money)?
  • Are there hidden or unexpected costs?

Our Research Process

Primary Research

  • Direct communication with program representatives
  • Review of program websites, terms of service, and pricing
  • Analysis of provider credentials and licensing
  • Evaluation of customer testimonials and reviews (when available)

Secondary Research

  • Review of medical literature and clinical evidence
  • Analysis of regulatory actions or complaints
  • Comparison of programs based on objective metrics
  • Follow-up research on program updates and changes

What We Don't Do

  • We don't accept paid placement or sponsorships from programs
  • We don't guarantee specific program performance or outcomes
  • We don't provide personal medical advice—always consult your healthcare provider
  • We don't recommend programs based solely on commission rates
  • We don't publish fake testimonials or artificial reviews
