How We Compare Programs

GLP-1 Health Connect is operated by Cherry and Cherry LLC. We publish an affiliate-funded comparison of selected telehealth programs. We are not a medical provider.

What this comparison covers

The homepage compares SkinnyRx and HumeCare+ using their publicly available offer pages and terms. It does not cover every provider or rank clinical quality. Placement is not a recommendation that one treatment or provider is right for you.

Sources and dates

The homepage offers were checked on October 8, 2026 against SkinnyRx, HumeCare+, and HumeCare’s terms. Details may change. The check date does not certify credentials, pharmacy quality, treatment outcomes, or the accuracy of every provider claim.

What to compare

  • Introductory cost versus renewal cost, billing interval, and total commitment.
  • The exact medication and whether it is FDA-approved or compounded.
  • Consultations, shipping, lab work, and other potential fees.
  • State availability, follow-up support, and cancellation terms.

How we are paid

We earn commissions when visitors enroll through our paid referral links, at no extra cost to the visitor. This is a selection of affiliate partners, not a comprehensive market survey. See our affiliate disclosure.

Limitations

We have not independently tested these services, verified every clinician’s license, or audited their pharmacies. Medical decisions belong to a licensed clinician. Always confirm the final offer with the provider before paying.

Medication information

Our homepage safety note refers to FDA guidance on unapproved GLP-1 drugs. It is general information and does not replace individual medical advice.
