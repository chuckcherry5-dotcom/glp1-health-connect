About GLP-1 Health Connect

Our Mission

GLP-1 Health Connect exists to provide transparent, independent research on GLP-1 telehealth programs. Our goal is to help people make informed decisions about their healthcare by providing accurate, evidence-based information without bias or hidden agendas.

Our Story

We created this resource because navigating the GLP-1 telehealth landscape is overwhelming. Programs make claims that are hard to verify, pricing is hidden until checkout, and quality varies significantly. We believed there should be a resource that evaluated programs honestly—not based on commission rates, but on what actually matters: safety, transparency, quality, and cost.

Our Core Values

Independence: We evaluate programs based on objective criteria, not on how much they pay us. Affiliate commissions do not influence our recommendations or rankings.
Transparency: We're honest about our affiliate relationships and explain how we make money. We disclose our methodology and invite scrutiny.
Accuracy: We source information from medical literature, official program websites, and direct communication with providers. We fact-check our content and correct inaccuracies quickly.
Empathy: We remember that people using GLP-1 programs are making important health decisions. We provide information without judgment and recognize that different options work for different people.
Safety First: Medical oversight, proper screening, and honest side effect communication are non-negotiable. We prioritize your safety over program popularity.

What We're NOT

We are not:

  • Medical professionals—we provide information, not medical advice
  • Employees or representatives of any GLP-1 program
  • A substitute for conversations with your healthcare provider
  • A source of testimonials or unverified user reviews
  • Profit-driven in a way that compromises our integrity
