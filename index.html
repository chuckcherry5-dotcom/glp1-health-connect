The Process

How Online GLP-1 Telehealth Works

GLP-1 Health Connect does not prescribe medication. We explain the general telehealth process so you know what to expect from providers.

1
Research and compare
Use this site to compare providers by price, medication options, and support before committing to any program.
2
Complete the questionnaire
Each provider collects your health history through an online intake form, typically covering weight, health history, and goals.
3
Provider review
A licensed healthcare professional reviews your profile and determines whether treatment may be appropriate for you individually.
4
Prescription and delivery
If prescribed, medication is fulfilled through the provider's pharmacy process and delivered directly to your door.
5
Ongoing support
Quality programs provide follow-up consultations, dose guidance, and ongoing patient support throughout treatment.
